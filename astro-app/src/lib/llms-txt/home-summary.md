UHC Watch is a public dashboard from the WHO Barcelona Office for Health Systems Financing. It monitors progress toward universal health coverage (UHC) across Europe and Central Asia.

The site tracks four dimensions of UHC — unmet need for health care, financial hardship, out-of-pocket spending on health, and coverage policy — with over 50 indicators drawn from harmonised national surveys and administrative data.

It is aimed at policy makers, researchers, journalists, and civil society. Each country page links to comparative analyses, country reports, and primary data sources so readers can move from a headline number to the underlying evidence.

The site is built and maintained by the WHO Barcelona Office together with the European Financial Protection Network. See the About page for the team and the Methods page for how indicators are defined and sourced.
